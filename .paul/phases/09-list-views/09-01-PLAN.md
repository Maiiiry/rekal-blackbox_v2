## Gradient Spotlight — Rata-rata Laba card
Ganti stat-card biasa menjadi gradient spotlight (violet):
```
background: linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(106,76,245,0.15) 0%, rgba(106,76,245,0.05) 100%)
border: 1px solid rgba(106,76,245,0.25)
```
atau menggunakan Tailwind: `bg-gradient-violet/10 border-gradient-violet/25`
